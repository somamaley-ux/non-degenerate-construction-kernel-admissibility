# Reference manuscript and Lean correspondence

**Reference:** v51.2, 25 September 2026. **Lean companion:** v0.3.2. The supported entry point is [`AASCKernelPaperClean.lean`](AASCKernelPaperClean.lean).

The repository formalizes the mathematical necessity chain developed around Proposition 2.4 and the subsequent reconstruction and consequences. The starting data are independently specified original subjects, complete uses, dependent witness types, an incidence relation `J`, and an actual witness where positive nonvacuity is required. They contain no A1–A4 certificate, kernel-governance flag, verdict-agreement field, or assertion that a proposed reader is sound.

## Necessity chain and its interpretation

1. **Original incidence and qualification.** `IncidenceNecessity` derives qualification from an actual witness. It constructs the complete semantic assessment and proves its exact relation to original qualification. A settled negative answer excludes every original witness; silence supplies no such proof. Multiple result values may be incident even though qualification has one Boolean answer.
2. **Identity, occurrence, and original warrant.** Identity probes separate independently identified semantic subjects. Uniform transfer of every original predicate is possible exactly under original identity; a particular transfer between different objects may instead follow from a separately justified domain theorem. Retaining original occurrence, conditions, and target preserves the original incidence. Later success on changed data cannot change an original failed qualification.
3. **Universal realization criterion.** `NecessityRealization` constructs an exact relational reader precisely when code fibres preserve the independently fixed original observation family. It proves uniqueness on the realized image and extracts omission, surplus, and fibre-collision witnesses against unsuccessful candidates. Identity retention is equivalent to unique reconstruction of the original subject on that image. This criterion ranges over arbitrary encodings; it does not assume their exactness as objecthood data.
4. **Constructed kernel realization.** `ObjecthoodNecessity` constructs a regime from `J`, proves that its standing and admission express original qualification, derives its represented four-role profile, and assembles the necessity and universal realization results. Its authorization bridge extracts an actual incidence witness at a finite semantic cut and supplies that cut's constructed kernel realization.
5. **Conservation and structural consequences.** The remaining modules derive warranted-answer conservation, quotients and typed composition, licensed construction, comparison, and grounded support under their explicit original-domain premises.

The mathematical correspondence to A1–A4 uses this complete chain of identity, qualification, retention, and exact-realization results. A `DerivedKernelRoles` record alone does not discharge that correspondence. Proposition 2.4 separately defends the constitutive passage from actual identity-bearing incidence to the target commitments; Theorem 3.1 reconstructs the functions performing their work. The reconstruction and the checked semantic consequences do not establish their own conceptual premises. A particular empirical identification or physical law is established in its domain. No optional `kernelApplies` premise is introduced.

Instantiation means performance of the required work in actual realization. For the realized incidence, Admissibility concerns its original conditions obtaining jointly; a faithful use or representation remains answerable to those conditions. A4 is the obligation of any claim offered about the incidence, without asserting the existence of a claimant, observer, or checking process. The semantic assessment represents qualification already fixed by the original relation.

The four roles identify functionally indispensable work in the same complete construction. Their coverage may overlap and their implementation may be compressed. Functional non-omissibility does not establish a unique partition of that work or logical independence of four axioms. The exact-realization and quotient theorems instead establish precise uniqueness for a fixed original observation family. Relevant questions omitted from an asserted complete frame require that completeness claim to be renewed against the richer original task.

## Interpretation, remaining written specialization, and application

The necessity construction is checked mathematics. Its semantic use starts from the original subject and relation, not from a proposed representation's label or desired answer. The manuscript defends that interpretation for actual non-degenerate determinate objecthood. Domain-specific physics, original bearer and query identifications, and correctness of any claimed concrete interpretation remain accountable to their own evidence; these are not an additional permission for kernel governance.

The numeric metric clause in Appendix B.9 follows from the checked binary classification and the ordinary metric axioms; its short real-valued specialization is written rather than separately declared in Lean. A chosen primitive language still needs proof that it generates the relevant observations or comparison paths. Finite congruence and finite authorization results do not by themselves prove infinite-use preservation, arbitrary infinitary authority, or liveness.
