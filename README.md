# Non-Degenerate Construction and the Kernel of Admissibility

Lean support for the **v50 reference manuscript**, released as **v0.2.0**.

The manuscript argues that non-degenerate determinate objecthood necessarily incurs **Admissibility, Standing, Reference, and Irreversibility**. This includes actual unobserved incidences. The kernel is constitutive of such objecthood; it is not an optional interpretive overlay on independently complete physics.

This repository checks the mathematical reconstruction and consequences of that argument: independently warranted result conservation, finite semantic authorization cuts, canonical observation quotients, equality-pattern classification, licensed construction, and grounded support. The formal proofs expose their premises. Declaring an incidence relation does not by itself prove the manuscript's constitutive passage from actual objecthood to the four roles (Proposition 2.4).

## Principal checked results

- **Conservation for arbitrary dependent query families.** Equal independently identified bearer and complete query, a determinate original result relation, and each answer's own incidence warrant imply equal answers. No pairwise answer agreement is supplied as a premise. The query family need not be finite or generated by a selected grammar.
- **Finite semantic authorization.** Every authoritative endpoint of a finite acyclic support graph has a globally minimal authoritative ancestor: authority occurs at a source or enters through an inference. Such a semantically governance-free finite certificate cannot yield an authoritative conclusion. The interpretation and warrant relation are explicit.
- **Canonical complete classification.** Equality of original observation profiles defines a quotient with a unique factorization property. Exact surjective classifiers are uniquely isomorphic over the original inputs. This is uniqueness of classification, not uniqueness of an occupant.
- **Equality-only finite interfaces.** Two finite tuples over any carrier are related by a carrier permutation exactly when their equality patterns agree. Every permutation-invariant observer factors uniquely through those patterns. Partition realization has an exact block-count criterion; binary observers have a unique diagonal/off-diagonal form; a nontrivial carrier has no fully permutation-invariant strict total order or fixed selector. Full permutation symmetry is an explicit additional hypothesis.
- **Licensed construction and generated closure.** Typed finite-arity partial operations retain their original graphs; licensed applications have unique outputs. The generated region is the least closed extension of its seed and equals the union of its finite stages, including nullary construction.
- **Grounded support and retention.** Support is generated from local checks and every required predecessor. It is equivalent to the checks on all ancestors, implies standing under verified local rules, and is preserved when an extension retains old checks and the complete old dependency lists.

The five manuscript extensions also prove canonical status and consumer invariance, exact primitive descent, preservation for a specified typed finite language, and an independently defined proof checker's soundness, completeness, and determinacy.

## Verify

Install the Lean toolchain selected by `lean-toolchain` and PowerShell 7 (`pwsh`). Then, from the project root:

```text
lake exe cache get
pwsh -NoProfile -File scripts/check-kernel-paper.ps1
```

`lake build` imports the original API, all five extensions, and the current `KernelReference` modules. The audit additionally checks source hygiene, the original semantic examples, exact named anchor reports, and the transitive axiom dependencies of all enumerated public project theorems. Missing reports fail the check. Only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` are permitted; no project axiom or placeholder dependency is accepted. The machine-readable result is written to `validation/run/audit.json`.

A successful build establishes the stated formal implications. Applying them requires the actual original bearer, complete query, warranted results, observation family, operation graphs, or complete dependency graph specified by the relevant theorem. The constitutive claim and those application identifications are explained in the manuscript and proof map.
